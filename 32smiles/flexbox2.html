General Dentistry

We provide complete preventive and routine dental care for patients of all ages. Regular check-ups help detect problems early and maintain long-term oral health. Our General Dentistry Includes: • Detailed oral examination and consultation • Digital X-rays for accurate diagnosis • Tooth-colored (composite) fillings • Gum disease evaluation • Preventive dental care plans • Oral hygiene education We recommend visiting every 6 months to maintain healthy teeth and gums.

Teeth Whitening

Over time, teeth can become stained due to tea, coffee, smoking, or aging. Our professional whitening treatments safely restore brightness. Benefits of Professional Whitening: • Noticeable results in just 45–60 minutes • Safe and enamel-friendly procedure • Removes deep stains • Boosts confidence instantly • Long-lasting results with proper care Achieve a visibly brighter smile in just one session.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Why Choose Dental Implants? • Prevents bone loss teeth. Why Choose Dental Implants? • Prevents bone loss • Restores chewing ability • Natural appearance • Long-lasting (10–20+ years with care) • No damage to adjacent teeth We use advanced implant technology for precision and comfort.

Root Canal Treatment

If a tooth becomes infected or severely decayed, root canal therapy can save it. Our Root Canal Procedure Includes: • Pain-free treatment using modern anesthesia • Removal of infected pulp • Deep cleaning and disinfection • Sealing and restoration with crown if required This treatment eliminates pain and prevents tooth extraction.

Smile Makeover (Cosmetic Dentistry)

Transform your smile with customized cosmetic treatments designed to improve appearance and symmetry. Smile Makeover May Include: • Porcelain veneers • Dental bonding • Teeth reshaping • Gum contouring • Whitening treatments We design smiles based on facial structure and patient preference.

Braces & Clear Aligners (Orthodontics)

Straight teeth are not only attractive but also easier to clean and maintain. Options Available: • Traditional metal braces • Ceramic braces • Clear aligners (invisible braces) Treatment duration typically ranges from 6–24 months, depending on case complexity.

Pediatric Dentistry

We provide gentle and friendly dental care for children in a stress-free environment. Services for Kids Include: • Preventive fluoride treatments • Dental sealants • Cavity treatment • Early orthodontic evaluation • Oral hygiene guidance We ensure your child feels comfortable and safe.

Crowns & Bridges

Restore damaged or missing teeth with durable and aesthetic restorations. Advantages: • Improves chewing function • Restores natural appearance • Strengthens weak teeth • Custom-made for perfect fit Materials include porcelain, ceramic, and zirconia.
