Faculty & Dental Specialists

At our dental clinic, experienced faculty members provide expert diagnosis, treatment planning, and patient care using modern dental practices. Our Faculty Team Includes: • General Dentist • Orthodontist (Braces Specialist) • Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon • Prosthodontist (Crowns & Implants Specialist) • Pediatric Dentist • Dental Hygienist • Dental Assistants Our skilled team ensures safe and high-quality treatment for every patient.

Clinical Support Staff

A strong clinical support team helps maintain smooth workflow and patient comfort throughout the treatment process. Support Staff Includes: • Dental Assistants • Sterilization Technicians • Reception & Front Desk Staff • Patient Coordinators • Cleaning & Maintenance Staff They ensure proper patient care and clinic management.

Modern Dental Equipment

Our clinic uses advanced dental technology for accurate diagnosis and comfortable treatment. Equipment Resources: • Dental Chair Units • Digital X-Ray System • Intraoral Camera • Ultrasonic Scaler • Dental Handpieces • Autoclave Sterilization Machine • Suction & Air Compressor System Modern equipment helps improve precision and efficiency.

High-quality instruments are used for safe and effective dental procedures. Common Instruments Include: • Mouth Mirrors • Dental Explorers & Probes • Forceps & Elevators • Scalers & Curettes • Surgical Instruments • Impression Trays All instruments are properly sterilized before use.

Materials & Clinical Supplies

We use reliable dental materials to ensure long-lasting treatment results. Materials Include: • Composite Filling Materials • Dental Cement • Impression Materials • Crowns & Bridge Materials • Gloves, Masks & PPE • Cotton Rolls & Gauze • Disinfectants Quality materials improve treatment success and safety.

Digital & Administrative Resources

Technology helps manage patients and improve clinic efficiency. Administrative Resources: • Patient Management Software • Digital Record System • Computer & Printer • Appointment Scheduling System • Internet & Communication Tools These systems ensure smooth clinic operations.

Patient safety and hygiene are our top priorities. Safety Measures Include: • PPE Kits • Sterilization Protocols • Hand Sanitization Stations • Biohazard Waste Disposal • First Aid & Emergency Kits Strict hygiene standards maintain a safe clinical environment.

Training Resources Include: • Clinical workshops & seminars • Continuing dental education (CDE) programs • Case study discussions • Practical demonstrations • Research & academic guidance Regular training helps maintain high clinical standards.
